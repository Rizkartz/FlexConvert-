Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We do not require user accounts or collect personal information. We only receive the files you voluntarily upload for conversion.

2. File Security

Your files are stored in isolated temporary directories. They are never shared, sold, or accessible by third parties.

3. Automatic Deletion

All uploaded and converted files are permanently wiped from server memory/storage after the session finishes or within 1 hour maximum.

4. Local Storage

We use browser sessionStorage only to keep track of your conversion history in the current tab. This data never leaves your browser.
